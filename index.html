About FikrLabs

“Fikr” means thought. We build on it.

FikrLabs is a new IT services company based in Ongole, Andhra Pradesh. We started with a simple belief: every good product begins as an idea, and every idea deserves careful engineering.

Institute

Android app + Web · Institute management

Run attendance, classes, students, fees and staff for your institute from your phone or the web. Built for schools, madrasas and maktabs, coaching centres and tuition classes.

  • Class, date and period-wise attendance with a monthly calendar
  • Dashboard with attendance graphs, plus PDF and Excel reports
  • Student records with photos, classes, sections, subjects and staff
  • Fees with Cash/UPI receipts and dues tracking
  • ID cards from ready templates, as PDF or Word
  • Roles and permissions, with teacher and student dashboards
  • Class-start reminders with a WhatsApp button for parents
  • English and Urdu (right-to-left), your logo and theme colour

Gym Manager

Android app · Gym membership management

Replaces the paper admission card. Keep every member, plan and payment of your gym in one place, see who is due at a glance and send reminders and receipts on WhatsApp.

  • Home at a glance: members, active, due in 7 days and overdue
  • Member profiles with admission number, photo and payment history
  • Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, annual and custom plans
  • Cash or UPI payments, with extra money kept as credit
  • One-tap WhatsApp reminders, welcome messages and PDF receipts
  • Bulk import members from Excel (.xlsx) or CSV
  • Your gym’s logo and colour across the app and receipts
  • Multiple gyms on one account, Google or email sign-in
